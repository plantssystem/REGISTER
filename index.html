Bits → sinesAn 8-bit LFSR steps on every 16th note. Each bit gates one sine voice, spaced by √2. 1ビットが1本のサイン波。

Number → pulseWhen bits 0 or 2 flip, a click fires at 3–17 kHz. The value itself decides the pitch. 数値がそのまま周波数になる。

Sub & reverbA 41 Hz sub kick on every 16th. One long-reverb note (RT60 about 7 s) lands somewhere in every 2 bars. 超低域の16ビートと、2小節に1音のロングリバーブ。

ScreenThe tesseract's pose is the number. Left column: LFSR value. Right column: raw counter. Barcode: last ten steps. 立体の姿勢が数値。
